2A typo in your email

One wrong letter (like gmial.com or gmail.con) sends your ticket nowhere. Use Find my ticket. It shows the email we have, like ju****@gmail.com. If it looks wrong, tap Use a different email. An SSC officer checks it, then your ticket is sent there right away.

3Your payment isn’t verified yet

The ticket is only sent after the SSC confirms your payment. Find my ticket shows if it is still being checked. Paid in cash? Message the Treasurer, Ms. Jhodel Aranas, or the President, Ms. Anthony Sedoro.

4It was just sent

Tickets go out every 5 minutes. Wait a few minutes, then pull down your inbox to refresh.

5You’re looking in a different account

Many phones have more than one Gmail. Tap your profile picture in Gmail (top right) and switch to the email you used in your reply.

6Still nothing?

Don’t worry. At the entrance, tell the SSC officer your full name. They can find you on the guest list and check you in.
